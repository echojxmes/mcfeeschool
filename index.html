About the school

McFee School of Irish Dancing is a County Down dance school with classes in Newry and Rathfriland, led by teacher Charlene McFee.

The school is built around the tradition and culture of Irish dancing, giving dancers the chance to develop their skills, confidence, technique and a real passion for the art, in a friendly and welcoming setting.

The school has also taken part in local and regional cultural events, including the 2023 Pumps and Brogues Concert at Newry Town Hall and the Ulster Championships
